01 How are Funko products distributed across categories?

Funko's top licenses come from many types of fandoms.

NBA leads this sample with 72 Pop! figures, followed by The Nightmare Before Christmas (45) and Five Nights at Freddy's (39). Sports franchises such as NBA and NHL appear alongside film, television and gaming licenses, including Star Wars, Harry Potter and The Mandalorian. This mix shows that Funko's catalogue draws on different types of fandoms, from established entertainment franchises to newer gaming communities. However, the rankings describe the products recorded in this dataset, not Funko's worldwide sales or the overall popularity of each franchise.

Different product types are led by different franchises.

NBA is strong in Pop! and Vinyl GOLD, while Star Wars leads Apparel. This shows that popular licenses are not distributed evenly across Funko's product types.

Pop! makes up the largest share of Funko's product range.

Pop! accounts for 768 products, more than five times the number of Apparel products. The remaining product types are much smaller, showing that Funko's catalogue is strongly built around its core figure format.

Different fandoms suit different types of collectibles.

Sports licenses like NBA and NFL are strongly linked to figure-based products such as Pop! and Vinyl GOLD, where fans can collect their favourite players. Entertainment franchises spread more across products like keychains, plush, apparel and board games, giving fans different ways to show their fandom.

02 How global is Funko?

Europe shows stronger Funko fan interest.

Funko search interest is especially strong across Europe, even though the U.S. is a bigger market. This may be linked to Funko's strong retail presence and collector communities in Europe. It also shows that a bigger market does not always mean stronger fan interest.

Funko brings the brand to fans through events.

Most verified locations on the map are temporary conventions or fan events, while only a few are permanent Funko stores. Many also take place toward the end of the year, showing how Funko uses major events to reach fans across different countries.

03 What does Funko pricing look like?

Exclusive products are more visible at higher price levels.

Most products are non-exclusive, but exclusive-tagged items are more concentrated in the middle and higher price bands.

Apparel carries the biggest exclusive premium.

Exclusive-tagged Apparel has the largest average price difference (+$7.61), exceeding Plush (+$5.09) and Pop! (+$3.88). This suggests that the premium associated with exclusivity varies across product types, rather than following a fixed markup. Product design, licensing and limited-edition releases may help explain these differences.

Funko's pricing varies from fixed prices to wide ranges.

Funko uses different pricing patterns across product types. Pop!, Keychains and Plush have prices concentrated around a few common values, while Apparel and Board Games show wider price distributions. Vinyl GOLD follows a distinct two-tier pricing pattern at $10 and $25. This suggests that Funko's pricing is shaped by product type, with some categories following consistent price points and others offering a broader range of prices.

04 How has Funko changed over time?

Sales trend

Funko sales peaked in 2022, then declined.

Annual net sales reached US$1.323 billion in 2022, then fell to US$908.2 million by 2025 (31.3% lower). These are company-wide sales, not product-release counts.

Geography

Funko's sales mix varies by region.

The United States remains the largest market, but its reported sales fell substantially between 2022 and 2025. Europe accounted for a greater share of revenue by 2025.

Brand performance

From one baseline, three different sales paths.

All three brand groups start at the same 2021 index of 100. By 2025, Loungefly remained about 3% above its 2021 level, while Core Collectible fell about 12% and Other fell about 48%. The y-axis accurately shows sales relative to each group's 2021 baseline. Hover over any year's point to compare the actual net sales of all three groups.
